# Camera cold-start plan

Approved by root before implementation, based onfc6d289:

1. Persist proven manual max RGB/depth profiles and independent sync, depth,
   finite wait/reconnect arguments; preserve clip1.2m and authoritative launch arguments.
2. Validate pinned private201-source CPU texture repair, artifacts and actual child
   wrapper/SDK ELF-loader resolution once before creating any camera action.
3. Scope exact preload to the vendor camera group; retain other node environments.
4. Provide explicit private build/preflight wrapper actions, with no implicit builds.
5. Focused offline/native launch tests, full startup-cost benchmark, author-separated
   source/performance review, then root-owned integration. No hardware/restart here.

Limits: no C++/row_step change, no new supervisor, no physical qualification;
positive vendor wait bounds the normal no-device branch only.
